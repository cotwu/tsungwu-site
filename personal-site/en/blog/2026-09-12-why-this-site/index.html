Why this site exists

Most of what I read for research never makes it into a paper. A trial gets summarised in one line of a discussion section; the physiology behind it stays in a notebook. This site is that notebook, kept in the open.

The concept entries follow one rule: separate what is mechanistically established from what the evidence has actually shown, and from what remains open. Each entry has those three parts, every citation carries a DOI link, and entries are revised as the literature moves rather than left as dated posts.

The first entry is on critical closing pressure. More will follow on how intraoperative hypotension is measured and predicted.

Entries are written in English first; a Traditional Chinese version accompanies each page.
