# Tsung-Ta Wu, MD

I am an attending anesthesiologist at National Taiwan University Hospital Hsinchu Branch.
This site is a working notebook on **perioperative hemodynamics**, **causal inference**,
and **medical education** — the concepts behind one question: *when we say intraoperative
hypotension is harmful, what exactly have we shown, and what have we only assumed?*

## Focus

- Perioperative hemodynamics | How intraoperative hypotension is measured and predicted, and where the evidence chain from 2007 to today still has gaps.
- Causal inference | Counterfactuals, target trial emulation, and g-methods — the tools that decide whether an observational hemodynamic finding means anything.
- Medical education | Clinical skills, simulation, OSCE design, and the learning-theory literature behind them.
